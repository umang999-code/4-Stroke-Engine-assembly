# 4-Stroke IC Engine – CAD Assembly & Working

📌 Project Overview

This project presents the 3D CAD modelling, assembly, and working principle of a 4-Stroke Internal Combustion (IC) Engine.

The project focuses on understanding the major engine components, their assembly, and the operating cycle of a four-stroke IC engine using SolidWorks.



#  Objectives

- To understand the basic construction of a 4-Stroke IC Engine.
- To model major engine components using SolidWorks.
- To understand the relationship between individual components.
- To assemble the components into a complete engine model.
- To understand the four-stroke operating cycle.
- To develop practical knowledge of mechanical CAD modelling and assembly.

# Working Principle

A 4-Stroke IC Engine completes one complete operating cycle in four strokes:

1. Intake Stroke

The piston moves from TDC to BDC and the fresh charge enters the cylinder.

2. Compression Stroke

The piston moves from BDC to TDC, compressing the charge inside the cylinder.

3. Power Stroke

Combustion produces high-pressure gases that push the piston downward and produce useful power.

4. Exhaust Stroke

The piston moves upward and pushes the burnt gases out of the cylinder.

One complete cycle requires 720° (two revolutions) of crankshaft rotation.
